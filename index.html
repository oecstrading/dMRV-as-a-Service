GCFT Portal / Services

MRV-as-a-Service Catalogue

GCFT Portal is a full-service MRV provider. We handle everything from project registration to credit issuance. You focus on your asset — we handle the measurement, reporting, verification, and market access.

📋

Project Registration & Feasibility

We assess your asset, determine eligibility under Gold Standard or Verra, select the right methodology, and prepare the Project Design Document (PDD). Includes baseline setting, additionality demonstration, and stakeholder consultation.

From $15,000 · 8–12 weeks
🔬

Continuous Monitoring & Data Collection

We deploy IoT sensors, satellite analytics, drone surveys, and meter integrations to collect audit-ready data. Our digital MRV platform automates data validation, anomaly detection, and reporting.

From $3,500/month · per project
✅

Verification Coordination

We manage the entire verification process — selecting the accredited VVB, preparing the verification package, responding to findings, and securing the verification statement. You get a signed, audit-ready certificate.

From $8,000 · per verification cycle
🏦

Credit Issuance & Registry Management

We handle registry accounts, credit serialization, issuance requests, and retirement tracking. Your credits are issued under your name — we manage the infrastructure.

3% of credit value · on issuance
📈

Carbon Market Access & Offtake

We connect your credits to corporate buyers, sovereign funds, and Article 6 bilateral agreements. Our buyer network includes European utilities, Caribbean hospitality groups, and multilateral institutions.

5% of transaction value · on sale
🌍

Article 6.4 Authorization Support

We navigate the Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism for you — from host-country approval to Letter of Authorization (LOA) and corresponding adjustment tracking. This unlocks premium sovereign pricing.

From $25,000 · 12–18 months
💡
Bundled offering available. Asset owners who subscribe to the full MRV-as-a-Service package (registration + monitoring + verification + issuance) receive a 20% discount on total fees and priority access to our buyer network. Revenue share models are also available for projects that prefer no upfront cost.
